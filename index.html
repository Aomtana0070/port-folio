About

Building things that help people.

”

I'm Tanapisit Onsroi (Aom) — a student with a strong interest in programming, artificial intelligence, and innovation.

I got into technology early, especially programming and problem-solving through information technology, and kept building on that foundation through online courses and IT competitions.

One of my proudest moments was reaching the final round of ITClash by King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL). My project "Bright My Blindway (AI Cam)" also won First Runner-Up at the DRU Science and Technology Project Competition, then advanced to the Top 10 at FIET LAND 1.5, hosted by KMUTT.

My goal is to become a software developer who builds solutions with real social impact — especially technology that improves quality of life. Studying Information Technology at KMUTT is the next step toward that.
